# AeroOps

## The Operating System for Aviation

**Scheduling, dispatch, maintenance, training, and money — one continuous,
auditable motion. Run the operation, not the paperwork.**

---

### Why operators choose AeroOps

**One system, one truth.**
Your schedule, your fleet, your students, your money, and your compliance live
in one data model — not five tools and a spreadsheet that disagree with each
other.

**Money that reconciles itself.**
Dispatch closeout updates meters, ledger, and invoice in a single atomic
transaction. A flight that closed cannot silently go unbilled.

**Airworthiness the system enforces.**
Squawks ranked by impact, inspection countdowns that never surprise you, signed
return-to-service, and traceable parts — so "is it airworthy?" is a fact, not a
memory.

**Explainable by construction.**
Every conflict, readiness score, and insight shows its reasons, over an
immutable audit trail. Trust is the product.
