# AeroOps — Executive Summary

### The Operating System for Aviation

**AeroOps is the system of record a flight operation runs its entire day on** —
scheduling, dispatch, maintenance, training, and money as one continuous,
auditable motion. It is what airlines have had for decades, at a price and
polish a six-aircraft flight school can adopt in an afternoon.

---

**Who it's for.** Part 61 and Part 141 flight schools, flying clubs, rental
operators, university aviation programs, and corporate flight departments — the
owner-operator who is also the operations department, and the multi-role team
that grows around them.

**The problem.** Small aviation runs on whiteboards, dispatch binders, and a
patchwork of single-purpose tools that don't share data. The schedule doesn't
know the airplane is grounded; the invoice doesn't know the flight closed;
checkride readiness lives in the chief instructor's head. The incumbents
digitized the *forms* — not the *operation*.

---

### What ships today

- **Scheduling** — conflict-aware booking of aircraft, instructors, and students.
- **Dispatch** — release, then Hobbs/Tach closeout that updates meters, ledger,
  and invoice in **one atomic transaction**.
- **Maintenance** — squawks, work orders, signed return-to-service, auditable
  parts movement, fleet health.
- **Training** — students, instructors, endorsements, stage checks, and
  checkride readiness on the record.
- **Billing** — invoices, payments, and receivables built into the operation.
- **Reports & Executive view** — utilization, revenue, and productivity you can defend.
- **Mission Control** — a live, full-screen operations wall with an audit timeline.
- **Role-based dashboards + RBAC + immutable audit trail** across every seat.
- **Import Center** — map, test (a real rolled-back run), commit, and roll back,
  with presets for Flight Circle, Flight Schedule Pro, FlightLogger, and more.
- **Multi-tenant SaaS** with self-serve onboarding, a public API, an installable
  mobile PWA, and explainable AI insights that advise but never act.

*Roadmap (clearly future): email/SMS delivery, Stripe payments, live METAR,
cloud document storage, native mobile apps, and predictive operational
intelligence. These plug into seams already built.*

---

### Why it's different

- **One operating system, not five point tools** — a single data model, not a
  scheduler with add-ons.
- **Atomic dispatch → invoice** — a closed flight cannot silently go unbilled;
  revenue leakage is structurally impossible.
- **Explainable by construction** — every conflict, readiness score, and insight
  carries its reasons; the audit trail is a first-class surface.
- **Role-based** — the student, CFI, dispatcher, chief instructor, ops director,
  and owner each open their own AeroOps, personalized by permission.
- **Import with rollback** — switching costs, not features, are the real
  competitor; "test it, and roll it back if it scares you" removes the fear.
