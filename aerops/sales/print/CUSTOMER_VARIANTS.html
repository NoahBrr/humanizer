AeroOps — Customer Deck Variants

How to tailor the deck for each buyer. Each section names the workflows and outcomes to emphasize, the three hero screens to lead with, and a one-line value proposition. Feeds customer-specific variants of DECK_CONTENT.md.

Part 61 Flight School

Buyer: owner-operator who flies, instructs, and runs the business — often the entire operations department.

Emphasize:

  • Adopt it in an afternoon — no consultant, no back office, no manual.
  • Billing that happens by itself — Hobbs/Tach closeout produces the invoice atomically; nothing closed goes unbilled.
  • "What needs me today?" — the dashboard's Needs-Attention triage as the single morning glance.
  • "What did today make?" — revenue and receivables visible before you leave.
  • Onboard the next CFI in minutes — role-based access without an IT project.

Three hero screens: dashboard.png · billing.png · schedule.png

Value proposition: Everything your school touches in one place — and billing that reconciles itself the moment a flight closes.

Part 141 Flight School

Buyer: chief flight instructor / school operator accountable for an approved syllabus, stage checks, and FAA-auditable training records.

Emphasize:

  • The pipeline out of your head and into the system — who's ready for a stage check, whose endorsements are missing, who's cleared to test.
  • Readiness with reasons — every checkride-readiness answer shows its factors, not a bare yes/no.
  • Endorsements and currency on the record — not the morning-of-checkride spreadsheet scramble.
  • Defensible records on demand — an immutable audit trail under every training action for the FSDO conversation.
  • Instructor turnover survivable — students and records don't get orphaned.

Three hero screens: training.png · students.png · executive.png

Value proposition: Your training pipeline — readiness, endorsements, and checkride clearance — as an explained, auditable record instead of tribal knowledge.

University Aviation Program

Buyer: collegiate program director running a degree program that flies — cohorts of students, a large fleet, and an accreditor who wants an audit trail.

Emphasize:

  • Accreditation-grade audit trails — defensible records at every term boundary, without a week of prep.
  • Fleet utilization that survives a provost's scrutiny — executive reporting with the reasons behind every number.
  • Scale that stays fast — multi-tenant architecture built for hundreds of students and a large fleet.
  • Program-wide visibility — executive and reports views across the operation.
  • Honest roadmap note: cohort/term grouping depth and cross-location roll-up are on the roadmap (1.5) — position them as coming, not shipped.

Three hero screens: executive.png · reports.png · training.png

Value proposition: A defensible, auditable system of record for a degree-scale flight program — where fleet utilization and training records withstand accreditation and a provost's review.

Cross-variant guidance

  • Keep the core narrative identical (Slides 1–5, 13, 20–24) — only the hero module slides and the closing emphasis change per buyer.
  • Never present roadmap items as shipped; when a buyer's need touches a roadmap item (card payments, live METAR, cohort reporting, native mobile), name it as roadmap and pivot to the shipped capability that covers the need today.
  • Lead every variant with atomic dispatch → invoice and explainable by construction — they are the two claims no incumbent can match.
