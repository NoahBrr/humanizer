# Risks and Open Decisions

> **Status:** Proposed — Phase 8 Part 1 · **Date:** 2026-07-10 · **Lead roles:** Head of Product; Reliability/SRE Engineer; Aviation Accounting Specialist; Flight School Owner · **Part of:** Revenue Engine design set ([README](./README.md))

This is deliverable 16 of Phase 8 Part 1, and it carries deliverables 17–18 in §5. It consolidates every open question raised across the design set (docs 00–13) into one deduplicated, prioritized register of decisions that need a human answer before or during Part 2, a risk register with mitigations already designed into the set, an explicit deferred-scope list, and the compliance confirmations the spec requires.

Where a sibling doc raised the same question twice under different framings, this register keeps one row and cites all sources. Where [13-database-model.md](./13-database-model.md) already made a binding call (its §3 resolutions R1–R26), the question is **closed** and does not appear here — only genuinely open decisions do.

---

## 1. How to read this register

**Decision priority tiers (§2):**

| Tier | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **P0** | Gates Part 2 kickoff. Commercial, security-boundary, or architecture-shaping. Do not start the affected Part 2 slice without an answer. |
| **P1** | Must be decided during Part 2 before the named implementation slice ships. A safe interim default exists and is stated. |
| **P2** | A safe default ships as designed; revisit in Part 2/3 with real-operator feedback. Listed so the decision is visible, not forgotten. |

**Risk scales (§3):**

- **Likelihood** — chance of the risk materializing during Parts 2–3 and the first year of operation, given only the mitigations designed in Part 1: Low / Medium / High.
- **Impact** — Critical = customer money wrongly moved, cross-tenant exposure, or a safety-workflow regression; High = financial-record integrity or trust damage requiring manual remediation; Medium = operational toil or delayed revenue; Low = cosmetic.

### 4.2 Part 3

- STRIPE_TAX adapter behind the TaxCalculator seam (INTERNAL ships first).
- Financial Export: `FinancialExportJob`, `AccountingMapping`, accounting-category export (per-item `defaultAccountingCategoryCode` snapshots are the Part 1 seam); compensation export handoff (contractors-only first per D6).
- Dispute handling flows on the `Dispute` model; DISPUTED-review restriction wiring if D33 lands.
- Queue/cron adapter (Inngest or equivalent) enabling batch payment timing policies (D14) and any approval-window expiry.
- Payer charge approval, if D7 pulls it into Part 3 rather than Phase 9.
- Airman-currency engine integration for provable endorsement/currency restrictions (D38).

### 4.3 Deferred beyond Phase 8 (recorded, not dropped)

- Invoice merging — upward corrections on paid reviews append delta lines instead.
- Prepaid packages — `CustomerCredit.origin` generalizes later; no wallet semantics.
- Payer split responsibility; per-program/charge-category payer routing (future `PayerRoutingRule` table, not JSON); payer spending alerts.
- Write-off execution — `WRITTEN_OFF` stays a reserved enum value with no writer.
- Multi-currency — columns and per-document currency invariants are ready; Part 1 enforces org-currency-only at the engine.
- VAT/GST and tax-inclusive pricing; jurisdiction/rate databases and nexus logic are never in scope (doc 07 disclaimer).
- Instructor payout execution — `PAID` is added to `InstructorEarningStatus` additively if it ships (the 1099 externally-paid marker, D44, does not require payout execution).
- Org-defined customer types (D11), structural owner↔user links (D35), non-student payer linkage (D36).

### 4.4 Rejected permanently (not deferred)

- **Unrestricted wallet balances.** Credits are bounded by origin, expiry, and targeted application by design; there is no top-up, purchase, or cash-out path, and adding one requires reversing a design-level rejection via ADR, not a feature ticket.

## 5. Compliance confirmations (Deliverables 17–18)

### 5.1 Deliverable 17 — nothing is deployed

Nothing is deployed. Phase 8 Part 1 produces markdown design documents in `docs/architecture/revenue-engine/` and nothing else. `prisma/schema.prisma`, application source, `package.json`, and all configuration are unchanged. No migration is generated or run. No dependency is installed. No infrastructure, environment, or deployment target is touched.

### 5.2 Deliverable 18 — no live payments are enabled

No live Stripe charges are enabled, and none can be: no Stripe integration, SDK, API key, webhook route, or provider reference exists in the codebase today (confirmed by [00-current-billing-audit.md](./00-current-billing-audit.md) §10). The design sanctions **Stripe test mode only** for Parts 2–3; charging sits behind the `REVENUE_CHARGING` environment flag, which defaults off and whose only sanctioned value in this phase is `test`. Live mode is gated on the Part 2 threat-model approval required by spec Part M. No production emails are sent — no email provider exists in the codebase, and payer notifications are designed as in-app only.

### 5.3 Standing constraints — not weakened by this design

- **Tenant isolation** — every new model is org-scoped with a real Organization FK; the payer boundary starts queries from relationships and fails closed; Stripe Connect (one customer per payer per connected account) makes cross-org payment identity structurally impossible; org-snapshot capture/wipe/restore is extended for every new model.
- **Authentication** — unchanged. `authorizePayer()` is an additive boundary beside `authorize()` (src/lib/session.ts), catalogued in SELF_SERVICE_ROUTES with written reasons and rate limiting.
- **RBAC** — all new permissions are data in the `src/lib/permissions.ts` catalog under module gating; no hardcoded role checks; no `Role` enum growth — leadership roles ship as OrgRole templates.
- **Financial controls** — this design adds controls (immutable approved snapshots, append-only corrections, separation of duties, second approvals, structural idempotency) and removes none. Money remains Prisma Decimal with explicit ISO 4217 currency; no raw card or bank data is ever stored (principle 8).
- **Audit attribution** — every designed mutation calls `recordAudit` with actor and before/after values; financial records carry denormalized actor labels so attribution survives user deletion.
- **Existing aviation workflows** — the airworthiness safety gate is reused untouched as the non-overridable floor; financial restrictions are a separate configurable evaluator that never gates aircraft return, squawk filing, or grounding; operational closeout never waits on a payment provider; the dispatch board and the atomic guarded closeout claim (do-not-break rule 5) are preserved; spec "check-in" language is rendered as aircraft return / flight closeout per AVIATION_STANDARDS.

## 7. Appendix — Day One and Defaults (owner-facing)

Across the design set a new organization inherits roughly 60 org-level settings spread over five configuration stores (`DispatchPolicy`, `RevenueWorkflowPolicy`, `RevenueSettings`, the checkout-restriction matrix in [10-checkout-restrictions.md](./10-checkout-restrictions.md), and the Revenue Item catalog in [06-revenue-items.md](./06-revenue-items.md)) — and no owner will read five documents to onboard. This appendix is the single owner-facing statement of the effective day-one posture. It is a **Part 2 acceptance artifact** (the shipped defaults must match §7.1 exactly — see §4.1) and doubles as the **sales-demo script**: what a school sees before it configures anything.

### 7.1 Effective default posture

| Area | Day-one default | Where to change it |
|---|---|---|
| Aircraft pricing | Existing aircraft rates keep billing unchanged via the legacy fallback (`LEGACY_FALLBACK`, doc 05) — no pricing profile is required to fly and bill | Aircraft Pricing Profiles ([05](./05-aircraft-pricing-profiles.md)) |
| Instructor rates | Existing `Instructor.hourlyRate` carries over via the generated legacy BILLING profile; compensation is off — billing-only orgs produce zero earnings (doc 04, R9) | Instructor Rate Profiles ([04](./04-instructor-time-and-rates.md)) |
| Revenue Items | All 26 built-ins seeded with **empty amounts** (D29) — an unpriced item never fires, so no surprise fees appear on any review | Revenue Item catalog ([06](./06-revenue-items.md)) |
| Tax | Off: no tax rules configured, `isTaxable=false` on everything except RETAIL; reviews show no tax lines until the org sets a rate | Tax settings ([07](./07-tax-model.md)) |
| Payment timing | Charge-immediately on review approval where a saved method exists; manual charge and manual invoice always available; batch policies withheld until the scheduler lands (D14) | Payment timing policy ([09](./09-payment-timing-and-collection.md)) |
| Payment collection | Card-first, ACH off until org opt-in (D4); charging is entirely off until `REVENUE_CHARGING` is enabled — manual invoice + offline payment recording work with charging off (R15) | Org settings + platform flag |
| Checkout restrictions | The airworthiness safety floor is non-configurable; the financial rows of doc 10's restriction matrix default to WARN or off — nothing new blocks a dispatch on day one (D19 hardens this only by org choice) | Restriction policies ([10](./10-checkout-restrictions.md)) |
| Review workflow | A draft Revenue Review is auto-created at closeout; staff with `revenue.approve` approve it; **no one is charged at closeout** (R13) | Workflow policy ([03](./03-revenue-review-lifecycle.md)) |
| Payers | Students self-pay by default; payer linking is optional | Responsible payers ([11](./11-responsible-payers.md)) |
| Allocation & reporting | On automatically; no configuration | — ([12](./12-revenue-allocation-and-reporting.md)) |

### 7.2 The real decisions — at most five

A new school makes at most these decisions; everything else above ships as a safe default:

1. **Price the Revenue Items you actually charge** — a subset of the 26; empty items never fire (D29, doc 06).
2. **Tax on or off** — off by default; if on, set the rate(s) (doc 07, D5).
3. **Payment timing** — keep charge-immediately or switch to manual invoice (doc 09, D13/D14).
4. *(Optional)* **Link responsible payers** for sponsor- or family-billed students (doc 11).
5. *(Optional)* **Harden checkout restrictions** beyond the WARN defaults (doc 10, D19).

Aircraft rates and instructor rates are deliberately **not** day-one decisions — the legacy fallbacks carry existing numbers over unchanged (docs 04/05), so a school bills on day one exactly what it billed the day before.
